# Fresh database and free launch

Deploy the frontend and API together. `Database.initialize()` creates the complete schema and reference data on an empty database. It no longer upgrades older schemas or backfills historical records. This release assumes the planned database reset; do not point it at an older schema. No database tables are deleted by the application or by this change.

Leave all four product switches off in `/admin/configuration`: publishing subscriptions, paid events, paid venues, and paid platform services. Fresh installations default to this state. Existing settings are preserved on restart. Free events, groups, venues, and article publishing remain available; article subscription quotas are suspended during free early access. Subscription checkout, paid ticket sales, paid venue rates, and one-off platform purchases are rejected server-side. Do not configure live Stripe prices or enable paid switches for this launch.

The public policies now describe individual-operated free early access; commercial policy drafts are not published. The confirmed operator identity is in `config.local.js`, but production environment overrides must also be updated. Hostinger hosting and email are in the Netherlands and backups are in Lithuania; transfer arrangements remain review warnings rather than startup blockers. See the [early-access legal review](early-access-legal-review.md) for exact deployment values, outage recovery instructions, remaining checks and validation. Exercise email verification, password reset, account deletion, privacy requests, and moderation on the deployed environment.

Operating without a limited company is different from having no business-registration obligations. Sweden assesses business activity using independence, duration, and profit motive; an initial free period does not by itself settle whether an activity is a business. Check the intended model with Skatteverket before treating future commercial activity as a hobby. See [Verksamt: hobby or business](https://verksamt.se/en/setting-up/planning-your-start/hobby-or-business) and [setting up as a sole trader](https://verksamt.se/en/setting-up/choose-business-type/sole-trader). Personal-data responsibilities remain relevant to a public service: see [IMY: controllers and processors](https://www.imy.se/en/organisations/data-protection/this-applies-accordning-to-gdpr/data-controllers-and-data-processors/).

Validation includes schema statement/foreign-key ordering checks, application tests, a frontend production build, and browser checks with mocked APIs. Run a fresh-schema smoke test against the deployment's MySQL version before release; local statement tests do not execute MySQL DDL.

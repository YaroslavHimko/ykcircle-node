# YKCircle Community Guidelines

> Last updated: [DATE]

YKCircle exists to help people make real plans, meet through shared interests, create communities, and organize events.

These rules apply to profiles, groups, events, comments/messages, images, reviews, business listings, and organizer activity.

## 1. Be authentic
No impersonation, deceptive identities, fake businesses, or manipulation of reviews/attendance.

## 2. No illegal activity
Do not arrange, promote, sell, request, or facilitate illegal goods, services, or activities.

## 3. No exploitation or sexual abuse
Strictly prohibited: child sexual abuse material, grooming, sexual exploitation, sexual content involving minors, non-consensual intimate imagery, or threats to distribute such imagery.

Do not download or redistribute suspected illegal material. Report it through the Platform.

## 4. No violent threats or extremist recruitment
No credible threats, instructions intended to facilitate violent wrongdoing, or recruitment/promotion for terrorist or violent extremist organizations.

## 5. No harassment or stalking
No repeated targeted abuse, stalking, doxxing, threats, or coordinated harassment.

## 6. Hate and discriminatory abuse
Do not unlawfully threaten, harass, or incite hatred or violence against people based on protected characteristics.

## 7. No scams
No fake events, phishing, fake ticket sales, deceptive payment requests, impersonation of organizers, or misleading business listings.

## 8. Event rules
Events must be accurate about date, time, location, price, restrictions, and organizer. Events may require moderation before publication.

## 9. Images
Only upload images you have the right to use. Illegal sexual content, exploitative imagery, malware, impersonation/fraud images, and unlawful publication of private images are prohibited.

Images may be automatically screened and manually reviewed.

## 10. Groups
Private groups must remain private and must not be exposed through public navigation/search.

## 11. Reviews
Reviews must reflect genuine experience. Fake, incentivized misleading, coercive, or retaliatory reviews are prohibited.

## 12. Spam
No repetitive unsolicited messages, mass posting, scraping users for marketing, or unauthorized automation.

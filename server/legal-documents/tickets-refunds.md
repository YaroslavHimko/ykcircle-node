# YKCircle Ticket, Cancellation & Refund Policy

> **Draft template — align with the final seller/payment model before paid tickets launch.**  
> Last updated: [DATE]

## 1. Identify the seller
Before purchase, checkout must clearly identify:
- event organizer;
- legal seller;
- YKCircle's role;
- ticket price;
- platform/payment fees;
- total price;
- cancellation/refund terms.

Do not describe YKCircle as seller unless it is legally the seller.

## 2. Payment models

### Direct organizer payment
Participant pays the organizer directly, e.g. via organizer Swish. YKCircle may display instructions and track status but does not receive/hold funds.

### Payment-provider checkout
Payment is processed by [PAYMENT PROVIDER]. The checkout must identify the merchant/seller.

### External provider
Participant is redirected to an external ticket/payment service. Its terms may also apply.

## 3. Ticket/payment status
Possible statuses:
- reserved;
- awaiting_payment;
- awaiting_confirmation;
- confirmed;
- waitlisted;
- cancelled;
- refunded.

Opening Swish or another payment app is not proof of payment.

## 4. Manual Swish
Recommended flow:
1. show organizer number/QR;
2. participant pays;
3. participant selects `I have paid`;
4. status becomes `awaiting_confirmation`;
5. organizer verifies receipt;
6. organizer marks `paid`.

Automatic `paid` requires a trusted provider callback/API confirmation.

## 5. Organizer cancellation
If the organizer cancels, the seller must comply with applicable law and disclosed terms. YKCircle may assist technically without becoming the underlying seller.

## 6. Material changes
Changes to date, time, location, programme, or major access restrictions should be communicated promptly. Legal/contractual refund rights may apply.

## 7. Participant cancellation
Display the organizer's cancellation rules before purchase. Policies may include refund deadlines, partial refunds, transfers, or no voluntary refund, subject to mandatory consumer law.

## 8. Right of withdrawal
Online purchases generally have withdrawal rules, but important exceptions exist. Services related to leisure activities for a specific date or period can fall within an exception to the general right of withdrawal.

Do not use a universal statement such as `all tickets are non-refundable` or `all tickets have a 14-day withdrawal right`.

Obtain Swedish legal review for the actual event/ticket categories sold.

## 9. Fees
Before payment show:
- ticket price;
- YKCircle fee;
- payment fee if charged to buyer;
- taxes where applicable;
- total payable amount.

## 10. Refund records
Track:
- order ID;
- payment ID;
- amount;
- currency;
- reason;
- initiated by;
- timestamp;
- provider refund ID;
- status.

Do not show `refunded` as complete until the payment provider confirms it when integrated.

## 11. Disputes/chargebacks
Track disputes separately from refunds. Organizer balances/payouts may need adjustment.

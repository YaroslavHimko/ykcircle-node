# YKCircle Venue Owner & Booking Terms

> **Draft for legal review before commercial venue-booking launch.**  
> Last updated: 7 September 2026

These Terms supplement the YKCircle Terms of Service for Venue organizations and users booking Venues.

## 1. Venue ownership and authorization

A Venue is controlled through the relevant YKCircle Organization and its authorized members/roles.

A Billing Profile does not grant ownership of a Venue.

Venue organizations must ensure only authorized users can edit Venue details, manage availability, view booking requests, confirm/cancel bookings, issue invoices and mark invoices paid.

## 2. Venue listing accuracy

The Venue organization is responsible for accurate information including, where applicable, name, description, address/location, capacity, amenities, accessibility information, prices, booking mode, availability, cancellation conditions, images/gallery and licences/permissions.

Do not upload images that materially misrepresent the Venue.

## 3. Venue timezone

Venue availability is interpreted in the Venue's canonical timezone.

Owners must check that location/timezone is correct.

Travel by the organizer or Venue owner does not change the Venue's scheduling timezone.

## 4. Availability

YKCircle may support recurring weekly availability, multiple windows per day, date-specific exceptions, blocked periods, external/manual reservations, confirmed YKCircle bookings and hourly/daily booking.

Availability is calculated dynamically.

A displayed free interval can become unavailable before confirmation.

## 5. Booking requests

A booking request is not a confirmed reservation.

Pending requests may overlap under the product's request model.

When a Venue owner confirms a request, the backend must re-check conflicts.

Only confirmed occupancy should block the applicable interval under the configured model.

## 6. External/manual reservations

Venue owners must record external/manual reservations where necessary to avoid double booking.

Public users should only see that the time is unavailable, not private reservation details.

## 7. Buyer Billing Profile

For an invoiceable/paid Venue booking, the organizer must provide an authorized buyer Billing Profile.

The buyer may be an individual or an Organization.

A company is not required merely to rent a Venue.

## 8. Seller Billing Profile

The Venue organization must maintain an appropriate seller Billing Profile before issuing invoices through YKCircle.

The seller Billing Profile may include legal name, organization number, VAT number where applicable, billing address, seller email and payment instructions.

## 9. Venue invoices

When a Venue invoice is issued:

```text
Seller:
Venue seller Billing Profile

Buyer:
Organizer-selected Billing Profile

YKCircle:
Software/invoice-delivery platform
```

YKCircle must not appear as the seller unless YKCircle actually supplies the Venue service.

## 10. Invoice review and issue

A draft invoice may be reviewed before issue.

Once issued:

- seller/buyer identity is snapshotted;
- invoice number is assigned from the seller's series;
- line items, tax, totals and issue date are frozen;
- the official PDF/accounting document is retained.

Do not silently edit an issued invoice.

Corrections should follow lawful void/credit/replacement processes as implemented.

## 11. Invoice numbering

Invoice numbers must be unique within the relevant seller's sequence/series.

Do not use a single YKCircle global number series for unrelated Venue sellers.

## 12. Payment

Unless integrated Venue payments are introduced later, the Venue seller receives payment externally according to its invoice/payment instructions.

The Venue seller may manually mark the invoice paid after actual receipt.

The organizer must not be able to mark their own Venue invoice paid.

## 13. Cancellation

A booking cancellation may release Venue availability.

If an invoice exists:

- an unpaid issued invoice must not simply disappear;
- a paid invoice must not be treated as refunded merely because the booking is cancelled;
- refund/credit treatment belongs to the Venue seller and applicable law/contract.

## 14. Venue seller responsibility

The Venue seller is responsible for providing the booked Venue, lawful operation, permits/licences, safety, insurance, accessibility obligations, taxes, invoice accuracy and applicable cancellation/refund obligations.

YKCircle does not guarantee Venue quality or suitability.

## 15. Organizer responsibility

The organizer is responsible for selecting appropriate booking times, accurate guest/Event information, complying with Venue rules, paying valid Venue charges and obtaining Event-specific permissions where required.

## 16. Private booking information

Private booking data, internal block reasons and external-reservation details must not be exposed publicly.

Only authorized users should receive detailed booking/invoice information.

## 17. Financial records

Issued Venue invoices may be retained after an account, Venue or booking is archived/deleted where necessary for accounting, disputes or legal obligations.

The Venue seller remains responsible for satisfying its own accounting-record requirements.

## 18. Platform tax reporting

Digital-platform tax-reporting obligations may apply to certain activities facilitated through a platform, including potentially relevant forms of property rental or services.

YKCircle and Venue sellers must provide information required by applicable law where such reporting applies.

This should be specifically reviewed before commercial Venue marketplace launch.

## 19. Suspension

YKCircle may restrict a Venue listing or booking functionality for fraud, fake listings, repeated double-booking/deceptive availability, safety concerns, legal violations, unpaid YKCircle platform fees where applicable or misuse of invoicing.

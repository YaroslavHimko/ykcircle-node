## 2. Data we may process
Depending on use of YKCircle, we process:
- account data: name, email, authentication identifiers, verification status;
- profile data: image, bio, city, languages, interests;
- community data: posts, group memberships, event participation, responses, reviews, images;
- organizer/business data: organization details, event details, attendee-management information;
- location data: selected city, venue coordinates, optional user-provided activity location;
- technical data: IP address, device/browser data, security logs, session identifiers;
- payment-related information only where a future approved payment feature is enabled; the pre-company beta does not receive or hold third-party ticket funds;
- support/moderation data: reports, appeals, correspondence, moderation decisions.

YKCircle should not store full payment-card numbers.

## 4. Public content and Google
Public posts, events, groups, profiles, business listings, and reviews may be indexed by search engines. The UI must clearly tell users when content is public and potentially searchable.

Private, members-only, draft, removed, or restricted content must not be intentionally indexed.

## 5. Images
Uploaded images may be resized, re-encoded, stripped of metadata, security scanned, automatically screened for safety, and manually moderated. Images may remain in restricted/quarantine storage while checks are pending.

If third-party image moderation is used, add it to the processor/subprocessor list.

## 6. Automated moderation
YKCircle may use automated systems to flag content for review. Where required, affected users must be informed about moderation decisions and available complaint routes.

## 7. Payments
The pre-company beta does not receive, hold, settle, or pay out money for third-party organizers. If paid functionality is introduced later, this notice and the applicable terms will be updated before it is enabled.

## 8. Sharing with organizers
When a user joins an event/group, the relevant organizer/admin may receive limited information such as display name, RSVP status, and payment status. Avoid exposing private email or phone data unless necessary and disclosed.

## 9. Service providers
We use service providers for hosting/database infrastructure, email delivery and media storage only as configured for the Platform. The current processor inventory, including locations and transfer safeguards, is maintained internally and is available on request through the privacy contact.

## 10. International transfers

## 11. Retention

Account-deletion requests are recorded, and content is removed or anonymised where eligible. Detailed retention periods are maintained internally and are available on request.

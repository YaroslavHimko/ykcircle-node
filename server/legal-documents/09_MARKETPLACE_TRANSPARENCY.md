# YKCircle Marketplace Transparency

> **Draft for legal review before commercial marketplace launch.**  
> Last updated: 7 September 2026

This page explains important marketplace relationships, seller identity, ranking and data access.

## 1. YKCircle's role

YKCircle connects users with Event organizers and Venue providers and supplies software for discovery, scheduling, booking and ticketing.

Unless explicitly identified as seller, YKCircle is not the seller of third-party Event tickets or third-party Venue rental.

YKCircle is seller of its own subscriptions and platform services.

## 2. Seller shown before purchase

For Event tickets, YKCircle should display ticket seller, organizer, declared trader/private-seller status, total price, cancellation/refund information and how responsibility is divided between seller and YKCircle.

For Venue booking, the Venue organization is the service seller unless otherwise stated.

## 3. Trader/private seller status

Sellers may be required to declare whether they act as a business/trader or private individual.

If a seller declares that it is not a trader, YKCircle may explain that consumer-protection laws governing trader-to-consumer contracts may not apply to that purchase in the same way.

YKCircle may request verification/supporting information where legally required.

## 4. Seller traceability

Where applicable marketplace law requires trader traceability, YKCircle may collect information such as name, address, phone/email, registration information, payment-account information, electronic identity/identification evidence and a self-certification of legal compliance.

The exact data collected must depend on YKCircle's applicable obligations.

Do not collect ID documents simply because the system has a Billing Profile if a legal requirement or verification need has not been established.

Payment-provider KYC and marketplace trader-traceability are related but not automatically identical obligations.

## 5. Search/ranking

Where YKCircle ranks Events or Venues, the main ranking parameters should be described in plain language.

Potential parameters may include only those actually used, such as relevance to search terms/category, date/time, distance/location, availability, popularity/engagement, freshness, quality/completeness signals, user preferences and moderation/safety restrictions.

Do not publish parameters that are not actually used.

## 6. Paid ranking

If YKCircle later offers sponsored/featured placement:

- label sponsored/paid placement clearly;
- explain to business users that payment can influence visibility;
- describe the relative effect of paid placement in ranking transparency information.

Do not implement hidden paid ranking.

## 7. Data access for business users

Organizer/Venue business users may receive data reasonably necessary for their Events, Venue bookings, attendee/order administration, invoices and analytics explicitly provided by YKCircle.

They must not receive unrelated user data or competitors' confidential data.

Business terms should explain what data they can access, what YKCircle can access, what remains available after termination and what may be shared with service providers.

## 8. Restrictions/suspension

Potential grounds include Community Guideline violations, fraud/scams, fake Events/Venues, unsafe activity, legal violations, payment/provider restrictions, repeated invoice/payment abuse, misuse of user data, intellectual-property violations and security threats.

Where required for business users, YKCircle should provide reasons and applicable notice/appeal procedures.

## 9. Changes to business-user terms

Changes affecting professional/business users may require advance notice under applicable platform rules.

Do not apply retroactive changes unless legally permitted.

## 10. Differentiated treatment

If YKCircle gives its own services or controlled businesses different treatment from third-party business users, describe the material difference where applicable.

If no differentiated treatment exists, state that accurately.

## 11. Platform fees

YKCircle may charge business users for its own software/platform services.

Fees do not make YKCircle the seller of the organizer's ticket or Venue service.

## 12. Tax/platform reporting

YKCircle may be legally required to collect and report information about sellers or transactions for certain platform-facilitated activities.

A separate assessment must determine whether and when these obligations apply to YKCircle's actual Venue, ticketing and other services.

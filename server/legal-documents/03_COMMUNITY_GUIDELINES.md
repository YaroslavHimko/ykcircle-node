# YKCircle Community Guidelines

> Last updated: 7 September 2026

YKCircle exists to help people turn interests and intentions into real-world plans.

These Guidelines apply to profiles, Groups, Group content, Events, Venue listings, galleries, messages, reviews, bookings, organizer/seller activity and other user content.

## 1. Be authentic

Do not impersonate people or organizations.

Do not create fake businesses, fake Venues, fake Events or misleading seller identities.

Do not manipulate reviews, attendance, bookings or reputation systems.

## 2. No scams or financial deception

Prohibited conduct includes:

- fake ticket sales;
- Events that the organizer does not genuinely intend to deliver;
- deceptive Venue listings;
- phishing;
- fraudulent invoices;
- false payment instructions;
- misuse of another person's Billing Profile;
- attempts to route payment outside agreed flows through deception;
- false seller/trader declarations;
- manipulating refunds or payment evidence.

## 3. No illegal activity

Do not use YKCircle to arrange, promote, offer, request or facilitate illegal products, services or conduct.

## 4. No exploitation or sexual abuse

Strictly prohibited:

- child sexual abuse material;
- grooming;
- sexual exploitation;
- sexual content involving minors;
- non-consensual intimate imagery;
- threats to distribute intimate imagery.

Do not download or redistribute suspected illegal material. Report it through YKCircle.

## 5. No violent threats or extremist recruitment

Do not post credible threats, instructions intended to facilitate violent wrongdoing, or recruitment/promotion for terrorist or violent-extremist organizations.

## 6. No harassment, stalking or doxxing

No repeated targeted abuse, stalking, threats, coordinated harassment or unlawful disclosure of private information.

## 7. Hate and discriminatory abuse

Do not threaten, harass or incite hatred or violence against people based on protected characteristics.

## 8. Event accuracy

Events must accurately describe, where relevant:

- organizer;
- seller;
- date;
- time and timezone;
- location;
- price;
- capacity;
- age/access restrictions;
- cancellation/refund terms.

Do not materially change an Event in a deceptive manner after users have booked or purchased tickets.

## 9. Venue accuracy

Venue listings must accurately represent:

- location;
- availability;
- capacity;
- amenities;
- price model where shown;
- booking conditions;
- images of the venue.

Do not list a Venue you are not authorized to represent.

## 10. Images and media

Only upload media you are entitled to use.

Do not upload illegal sexual content, exploitative imagery, malware, fraudulent identity/business imagery, unlawful private imagery, or media designed to deceive users about an Event or Venue.

Private/draft media must not be intentionally shared outside the intended audience.

## 11. Groups

Group content must follow these Guidelines.

Private Groups must not be used to evade rules that apply elsewhere on YKCircle.

## 12. Messages

No spam, phishing, sexual harassment, threats or abusive unsolicited messaging.

Do not scrape users for unrelated marketing.

## 13. Reviews

Reviews must reflect genuine experience.

Prohibited: fabricated reviews, review farms, deceptive incentives, coercive reviews, retaliatory manipulation and self-review through alternate accounts.

## 14. Payment and refund conduct

Sellers must not:

- claim payment was received when it was not;
- intentionally refuse a legally required refund;
- continue selling tickets when they know the Event will not be delivered;
- use YKCircle to evade payment-provider restrictions.

Users must not submit fraudulent chargebacks or payment claims.

## 15. Spam and abusive automation

No repetitive unsolicited posting, mass messaging, scraping, fake engagement or unauthorized automation that harms users or the Platform.

If there is an immediate threat to life or safety, contact the appropriate emergency authority.

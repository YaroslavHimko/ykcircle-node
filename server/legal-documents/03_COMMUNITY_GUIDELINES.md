# YKCircle Community Guidelines

> Last updated: 2 October 2026

These rules apply to profiles, events, articles, groups, venue listings, messages, invitations, bookings and reviews.

## 1. Be honest and respectful

Use accurate information. Do not impersonate others, create fake activities or manipulate attendance, ratings or reviews. Respect people's boundaries and their decision not to participate.

## 2. Keep activities accurate and free

Describe dates, timezones, locations, capacity, accessibility, house rules and participation requirements accurately. Update participants when plans change. Only offer a venue you are authorized to use. Do not misrepresent a compulsory charge as a free activity.

## 3. Protect people

Harassment, stalking, threats, hateful abuse and disclosure of another person's private information without a lawful basis are prohibited. Do not facilitate violence or recruit for violent extremism.

Sexual exploitation, child sexual abuse material, grooming and non-consensual intimate imagery are prohibited. Do not download or redistribute suspected illegal material to report it; provide the location or reference instead.

## 4. Share responsibly

Only post material you have the right to use. Do not upload malware, infringe copyright, publish unnecessary sensitive information or share restricted content outside its intended audience.

Reviews must reflect real experience. Articles and activity descriptions must not use deceptive claims or hidden promotions. Private groups follow the same safety rules as public spaces.

## 5. No scams, spam or abuse

Do not use YKCircle for phishing, deceptive requests for money, unsolicited mass messaging, scraping personal data, fake engagement, unlawful activity or attempts to bypass security controls. Invitation and participant details must not be repurposed for unrelated marketing.

## 7. Emergencies

YKCircle is not an emergency service and reports are not continuously monitored. Contact emergency services when there is an immediate danger to life or safety.

# YKCircle Organizer & Ticket Seller Terms

> **Draft for legal review before paid-ticket launch.**  
> Last updated: 7 September 2026

These Terms supplement the YKCircle Terms of Service for users who create Events or sell tickets.

## 1. Organizer identity

An organizer may act personally or for an Organization the user is authorized to represent.

Creating a free Event does not require a business, Billing Profile or Payment Account.

## 2. Organizer is not automatically the seller

Event management and financial seller identity are separate.

For a paid Event, the organizer must select an authorized **seller Billing Profile**.

The seller Billing Profile represents the person or organization financially acting as ticket seller.

The organizer must not select a Billing Profile they are not authorized to use.

## 3. Seller capacity

Before paid sales are enabled, the seller may be required to declare whether the seller acts:

- as a business/trader; or
- as a private individual.

This declaration must be truthful.

YKCircle may display the declared status to attendees and may request supporting information where required by law, fraud controls or payment-provider requirements.

## 4. Billing Profile

A paid ticket seller must maintain an adequate seller Billing Profile.

Depending on the seller and applicable requirements, this may include legal name, organization name, organization number, VAT number where applicable, address, country and public/support contact details.

A Billing Profile alone does not mean the identity has been independently verified.

## 5. Payment Account

Paid ticket sales require a supported connected Payment Account.

The Payment Account may be subject to identity/KYC verification, sanctions/fraud screening, payment-provider terms, capability requirements and bank/payout requirements.

Where provider-hosted onboarding is used, the payment provider collects required sensitive verification information directly.

YKCircle should not require organizers to upload raw identity documents to YKCircle unless a separate legal requirement and secure process have been implemented.

## 6. Direct-charge model

Where YKCircle's intended connected-payment architecture is enabled:

- attendee payments are created as direct charges on the seller's connected account;
- the connected account receives the payment balance;
- the payment provider manages payout to the seller;
- YKCircle does not collect the organizer's gross ticket proceeds for later payout;
- refunds and chargebacks affect the connected account according to the provider configuration;
- the organizer/seller manages refunds/disputes through the provider.

YKCircle may receive limited payment/refund/dispute status in order to update orders and tickets.

The actual provider configuration controls payment liability and must match these Terms before launch.

## 7. No YKCircle organizer payout service

Unless expressly introduced later, YKCircle does not provide an organizer wallet, a YKCircle payout schedule, pooled organizer ticket funds or YKCircle-managed ticket-revenue transfers.

Do not represent the Platform as holding ticket revenue for organizer payout if the direct-charge model is used.

## 8. Organizer responsibilities

Organizers are responsible for, where applicable:

- Event accuracy;
- lawful Event operation;
- Venue rights;
- safety and capacity;
- licences and permits;
- staffing;
- accessibility;
- insurance;
- music/public-performance rights;
- food/alcohol compliance;
- tax obligations;
- attendee communications;
- lawful marketing;
- cancellation and refunds;
- compliance with consumer obligations applicable to the seller.

## 9. Required Event information

Where applicable, Events must show:

- Event title/description;
- organizer;
- ticket seller;
- seller capacity/status;
- date/time/timezone;
- location;
- ticket price/currency;
- mandatory fees and total price;
- capacity/access restrictions;
- refund/cancellation terms.

## 10. Paid Event readiness

Organizers may create and edit a paid Event draft before financial setup is complete.

Ticket sales must not be enabled until required checks are satisfied, including:

- valid seller Billing Profile;
- authority to use the Billing Profile;
- seller capacity declaration;
- connected Payment Account;
- provider onboarding sufficiently complete;
- payment capability enabled.

YKCircle may disable new ticket sales if the Payment Account loses required capability.

## 11. Seller information shown to attendees

YKCircle may display seller information necessary to identify the seller and comply with marketplace/consumer obligations.

Do not provide misleading or false seller information.

If the seller is a private individual, the Platform may notify consumers that consumer-protection rules applicable to trader-consumer contracts may not apply to that transaction in the same way.

## 12. Ticket contract

Unless YKCircle is expressly identified as seller, the ticket contract is between:

```text
Attendee
↔
Seller identified at checkout
```

YKCircle provides software, discovery, order/ticket state and related services.

## 13. Order confirmation

After successful payment, YKCircle may provide the attendee with order number, Event, Event date/time, seller, ticket type, quantity, amount paid, refund-policy snapshot, ticket/QR code and payment status.

This record does not make YKCircle the seller.

## 14. Refund policy

Before ticket sales, the organizer/seller must configure an accurate cancellation/refund policy.

The policy may not remove mandatory statutory rights.

Changing the policy later must not silently rewrite the terms applying to earlier orders.

## 15. Organizer cancellation

If the Event is cancelled, the organizer must update Event status promptly, notify attendees, stop further sales, process refunds where required by law or applicable policy, and cooperate with payment-provider/dispute processes.

YKCircle may assist technically or disable tickets without becoming the underlying seller.

## 16. Material Event changes

Material changes may include date, time, venue/location, major programme change or material access restrictions.

Communicate material changes promptly.

Refund/cancellation rights may apply depending on law and contract.

## 17. Refunds

Where direct charges are used, refunds should be initiated by the seller through the connected payment provider.

YKCircle may synchronize the resulting state:

```text
paid
partially_refunded
refunded
disputed
```

A fully refunded ticket should no longer remain valid for admission unless a lawful/support exception applies.

## 18. Disputes and chargebacks

The seller must respond to disputes according to payment-provider rules.

YKCircle may display dispute status and restrict ticket functionality where necessary.

The Platform must not promise that it bears connected-account dispute losses unless the actual provider configuration says so.

## 19. YKCircle fees

YKCircle may charge organizers for subscriptions, one-time Event/platform service fees, premium/featured placement and other disclosed software/platform services.

These are separate from attendee ticket revenue.

For the initial model described in this pack, YKCircle does not charge a per-ticket application fee.

If a per-ticket fee is introduced later, these Terms, pricing, refund treatment and accounting implementation must be updated first.

## 20. Venue booking

An organizer may book a Venue personally or through an Organization.

A paid/invoiceable Venue booking requires an authorized buyer Billing Profile before the booking can become invoiceable/confirmed as required by the booking flow.

The ticket seller Billing Profile and Venue buyer Billing Profile may be different.

Do not assume:

```text
organizer = venue buyer = ticket seller
```

## 21. Attendee data

Use attendee data only as reasonably necessary for Event administration and lawful related purposes.

Do not sell attendee personal data.

Do not use attendee information for unrelated marketing without a valid lawful basis.

## 22. Platform data and rankings

Business users may be subject to the Marketplace Transparency page, which describes main ranking parameters, paid influence on ranking where applicable, data access, suspension/restriction grounds and differentiated treatment where applicable.

## 23. Fraud and abuse

Prohibited: fake Events, selling tickets to Events not intended to take place, deceptive pricing, misleading seller identity, manipulation of payment evidence, abusive chargeback/refund conduct, misuse of attendee data, attempts to evade provider restrictions, and using unauthorized Billing Profiles or Payment Accounts.

YKCircle may suspend ticket sales or organizer functionality where reasonably necessary for safety, fraud or legal compliance.

## 24. Taxes and platform reporting

The seller remains responsible for its own income, VAT and other tax obligations.

YKCircle may collect or report seller/transaction information where legally required.

Platform tax-reporting obligations, including any obligations relevant to digital-platform reporting, must be assessed for the specific activities actually facilitated by YKCircle.

## 25. Verification

YKCircle may use different states such as Billing details complete, Organization details validated, Payment Account ready, and seller/trader information verified where legally required.

Do not interpret one status as proving all others.

## 26. Termination

If organizer functionality is restricted or terminated, completed financial/order records may remain available/retained as required.

Professional/business users may have additional notice/reason rights under applicable platform law.
